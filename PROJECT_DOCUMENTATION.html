BizLens — Architecture & Functional Reference Guide

Enterprise Multi-Tenant Business Intelligence & Financial Analytics Platform

1. Executive Summary

BizLens is a cloud-native SaaS analytics platform designed to solve core data challenges for businesses:

  • Automated Ingestion & Sanitization: Cleans raw CSV sales and expense datasets through a 5-stage ETL pipeline.
  • Executive KPIs & Formulas: Instantly computes Gross Revenue, Net Profit, Profit Margin %, AOV, and PoP growth.
  • Automated Root-Cause Analysis: Identifies exactly which products, regions, and cost surges drove monthly profit swings.
  • RFM Customer Segmentation: Automatically classifies buyers into VIP, High-Value, Regular, At-Risk, and Churned cohorts.
  • Security & Audit Logging: Tracks every authentication attempt with IP, device fingerprint, and authorization state.

2. Core Modules & Functional Breakdown

A. Authentication & Security (backend/users/)

LoginView.post(request)

Significance: Gatekeeper for the platform. Validates user credentials, issues dual JWT tokens (access + refresh), and triggers immutable security logging.

Audit Logic: Creates a LoginRecord row for every authentication event (capturing status SUCCESS or FAILED along with detailed reasons: Account does not exist, Incorrect password, etc.).

get_client_ip(request)

Significance: Resolves the genuine client IP address through proxies and serverless gateways via HTTP_X_FORWARDED_FOR or REMOTE_ADDR.

LoginHistoryView.get(request)

Significance: Audit log inspection view. Enforces IsAdminUser permissions so that only superusers and staff can view login attempts (returns HTTP 403 Forbidden to regular users).

B. Multi-Tenancy & Isolation (backend/businesses/)

IsBusinessMember.has_permission(request, view)

Significance: Multi-tenant isolation firewall. Validates the X-Business-ID header and guarantees users can only access data belonging to organizations where they hold active membership.

BusinessListCreateView.get_queryset(self)

Significance: Allows a single user (e.g. an accountant or multi-brand founder) to switch seamlessly across multiple companies without data leakage.

C. Data Ingestion & Cleaning Pipeline (backend/uploads/)

process_sales_csv(file_obj, business)

Significance: 5-stage automated in-memory ETL pipeline for sales data:

  1. Deduplication: Eliminates identical sales records.
  2. String Sanitization: Trims whitespace and normalizes case.
  3. Numeric Coercion: Cleans currency symbols and converts to IEEE-754 floats.
  4. Date Normalization: Converts multiple date formats into ISO standard dates.
  5. Data Hygiene Scoring: Computes quality_score = (valid_records / total_records) * 100.

Executed inside a database transaction.atomic() block to ensure zero data corruption.

process_expenses_csv(file_obj, business)

Significance: Ingestion pipeline for OPEX costs, mapping categories to Marketing, Payroll, Rent, Logistics, and Utilities.

D. Financial Analytics Algorithms (backend/analytics/)

calculate_revenue_metrics(business, start_date, end_date)

Significance: Computes top-line performance indicators:

  • Total Revenue: Aggregated via Sum('total_amount').
  • Average Order Value (AOV): Total Revenue / Total Orders.
  • Growth %: Period-over-Period variance between recent periods.
  • Regional Distribution: Revenue contribution grouped by sales territory.

calculate_expense_metrics(business, start_date, end_date)

Significance: Computes bottom-line metrics:

  • Net Profit = Total Revenue - Total Expenses.
  • Profit Margin % = (Net Profit / Total Revenue) * 100.

calculate_customer_analytics(business)

Significance: Implements RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation:

  • VIP: High spend (≥ ₹1,00,000), ≥ 3 orders, purchased within 60 days.
  • High Value: Spend ≥ ₹50,000 within 90 days.
  • Regular: Active within 90 days.
  • At Risk: Lapsed (91–180 days since last order).
  • Churned: Inactive (> 180 days since last purchase).

perform_root_cause_analysis(business)

Significance: Automated diagnostic algorithm. Compares the two most recent monthly periods to explain why revenue or margins shifted, pinpointing the single largest declining product and underperforming region.

E. AI Business Intelligence Assistant (backend/insights/)

AskBusinessInsightView.post(request)

Significance: Natural language query engine. Interprets business questions (e.g. "Why did revenue fall?" or "Which product is best?") and responds with synthesized calculations.

F. Serverless Gateway & Runtime (api/index.py)

handler(environ, start_response)

Significance: The WSGI serverless adapter for Vercel:

  • Dynamic Path Discovery: Scans Lambda container directories to resolve imports.
  • Path Normalization: Restores HTTP_X_FORWARDED_URI to neutralize Vercel rewrite changes.
  • SQLite Bridge: Automatically copies pre-migrated db.sqlite3 to /tmp/db.sqlite3 for writable serverless execution.
  • Diagnostic Catching: Traps uncaught exceptions and displays exact tracebacks instead of generic crashes.
